# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People doing development and knowledge work with agents, using either local harnesses or a hosted durable agent.

## Product Purpose

Provide a small workspace for starting agent tasks, following progress, reviewing results, and returning to saved conversations. Keep runtime differences explicit when they affect the user's work.

## Brand Personality

Plain, concise, understated. Useful information takes precedence over personality or promotional language.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing language, whimsical empty states, all-caps labels, and architectural jargon where it does not help the user act. Do not imply that ephemeral VM files are durable.

## Design Principles

- Explain outcomes and next actions, not implementation details.
- Use consistent terms and sentence case; retain conventional acronyms.
- Keep local and durable-agent capabilities accurate and distinct.
- State destructive consequences plainly.
- Remove filler rather than replacing it with another slogan.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA. Preserve descriptive labels, keyboard access, readable contrast, and clear error messages. This is a target, not a claim of verified compliance.
